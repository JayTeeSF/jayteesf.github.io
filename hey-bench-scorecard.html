How to read the dots
0.5× ├──●──┤ 1× ├─────┤ 2×

The dot is the middle of 5 runs. Where shown, the line spans the smallest and largest values measured in those 5 trials.

On the C chart, each tick doubles. 1× is C’s median for that workload. Half the time on one workload (0.5×) and double the time on another (2×) balance to 1× in the geometric mean.
