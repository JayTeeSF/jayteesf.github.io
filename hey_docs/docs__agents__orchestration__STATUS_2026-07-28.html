THE FIVE THINGS THAT MATTER MOST

FINDINGWHY IT MATTERS
**1****Multi-core is broken and it is ours**Go scales to 4 cores (+23%); we scale 1%. The global-lock work now has a measured baseline instead of a guess
**2****The actor diet buys bytes, not speed**1BRC, web, and tight loops contain **zero actors**. Verified by grep, three ways. Do not fund the diet as a speed program
**3****Actors are never freed**No `free(actor)` anywhere in the runtime. Shrinking 192→64 converts an unbounded 258 B leak into an unbounded 137 B leak. A slab makes a reused actor cost **zero**
**4****The web path leaks 13.2 KB per request**The maximal actor diet saves 19.4 MB at 100K actors — **less than ~1,470 requests currently leak**
**5****Nine instruments were lying**Gates that could not fail; a ratio taken where one lane had headroom; a benchmark that could not tell "slow" from "wrong toolchain". Every one in an instrument, none in the product
**6****`a[i] = v` silently does nothing in the interpreter**Compiled fails CLOSED ("cannot lower"); the interpreter says "invalid name" and **leaves the array unchanged**. A user sees no error and gets a wrong answer. Two-lane divergence. Sliced as `wsetidx`, fail-closed half lands first
