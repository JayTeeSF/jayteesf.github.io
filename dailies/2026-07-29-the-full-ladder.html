The retirement ladder — R2 → R8

"Get rid of native C." Two separate deletions, marked red. Only groundwork is done; neither deletion has started.

  • R2 ~490alive rung

    Narrow the build surface: the Hey-owned emitter must accept every shape the old lane accepts. ~12–18 reds left, was ~30.

  • R3 ~515adeletion #1

    DELETES the native LLVM emitter — ~14,900 lines of heyc.c.

    R3.1 golden pinning done (393a) — deletion is possible. Helper split and batch deletion not started; needs R2 closed plus a conformance corpus that doesn't exist yet.

  • R4small · any time after R2

    Label Stage-0 honestly and add a checker that fails when anything grows a new native callsite.

  • R5 ~500agates R6 + R7

    Compile-first verification. The spec runner itself runs on the interpreter today — the gates must stop depending on the thing they'll certify the deletion of.

  • R6 ~530adeletes hey_repl.c

    REPL retirement: ORC-JIT session (the researched swing item, 5–8 agent-days), Eval moves onto it, the shell moves Hey-side, then hey_repl.c is deleted. Largest schedule risk on the board.

  • R7 ~540adeletion #2

    Bootstrap fixed point — Stage 1 rebuilds itself byte-identically without the native seed — then DELETE the Stage-0 native-C backend. This is the one that actually ends native C.

  • R8 ~550athe end · realistic ~Aug 14

    semantic_debt_files = 0. heyc.c is a thin launcher and linker; no language semantics live in C.

Past R8 — what the ladder is for

After the cruft is gone, the language program. Ordered by leverage, not by calendar.

1 · Names, finished ruled 2026-07-29

Zero reserved names anywhere: exposing-import everywhere, import Sys as the one-line ergonomic opening, warnings hardening per-code. Your data, your functions, your names — always.

2 · Recursion, guaranteed

Full tail-call depth in both lanes and a clean diagnostic where depth is real — the segfault becomes an error, the Erlang-style server loop becomes teachable.

3 · Multi-core web

The honest broken row: 8× workers currently buy 1%. Post-R8 this is the largest performance frontier — and the slice where the cross-box generator rig (Thunderbolt bridge or gigabit) earns its hardware.

4 · The bets

1BRC under 43 s on the headline file · CLI lane 23 ms → sub-5 ms · the p99.9 tail (1.77× Go — the allocation model's signature, fed by I5's copy-on-write ladder).

5 · The teaching surface

The new tutorial (drafted, oracle-verified, 26 chapters) and the human-facing doc rewrite become the public face — the first-hour-vs-Ruby test.

6 · Exit alpha

Packages and ecosystem maturity to the point where "no compatibility obligations" ends: the Ruby-replacement pitch — equal ergonomics, overwhelming speed — goes to strangers and holds.
