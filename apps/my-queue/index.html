Install

  1. Download the installer above.
  2. Open it. It installs for you only, needs no administrator password, and adds My Queue to the Start menu.
  3. Remove it any time from Settings, Apps, Installed apps (see How do I uninstall it?).

Check the download (optional)

In PowerShell, in the folder you downloaded to:

Get-FileHash MyQueue-Setup-0.2.1.exe

The Hash it prints must equal the fingerprint above (PowerShell shows it in capital letters; that is the same value).

Questions

How do I uninstall it?

  1. Open Settings, then Apps, then Installed apps.
  2. Find My Queue and choose ..., then Uninstall.
  3. Confirm with Uninstall, then Uninstall again in the My Queue Uninstall window. It removes the program, its Start menu entry and its entry in Installed apps.

Uninstalling keeps your sign-in and settings in %LOCALAPPDATA%\MyQueue, so installing again finds you still signed in. To remove them too, delete that folder after uninstalling.

Why does Windows warn me?

Windows checks who made a program before it runs it the first time. This test build is not signed yet, so Windows says Windows protected your PC and Unknown publisher. On your own PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Does it need an administrator?

No. It installs only for you, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\MyQueue, and changes nothing for other people on the PC.

Can I use it on a work computer?

Ask your IT department first. Their rules decide what may run. They may ask for the publisher (JayTeeSF LLC), the download address and the SHA-256 fingerprint above.
