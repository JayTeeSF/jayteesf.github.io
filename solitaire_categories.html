How to play

Every deal uses four secret categories. Word cards never tell you which one they belong to — that is the puzzle.

  1. Four key cards (gold, with a crown) are shuffled into the deck. Drop a key card on a locked slot to open that category.
  2. Once a category is open, send its words to that slot — one card, or a whole stack at once.
  3. In the tableau you may stack a card on another card only if they share a category. Use this to gather words while you wait for their key.
  4. An empty column takes any card. Empty columns are the scarcest thing on the board.
  5. Tap the stock to draw. Tap a card to pick it up, then tap where it should go. Dragging works too.
  6. You get two passes through the stock. After that it is spent, so do not leave cards you can still place sitting in the waste.

Guessing costs you. Aiming a card at the wrong slot or the wrong stack is a miss: −5 points, and the miss counter goes up. Nothing else is lost, so a considered guess is fine — brute force is not.

Finish with zero misses for a perfect-deduction bonus.

New to it? Turn on Practice mode in Settings to see every card’s category and drop the miss penalty.

The game supports tap play, drag-and-drop, autosave, export/import, and up to 25 levels of undo.

Hint names the best move and says why — it will spoil a category to do it, so use it when you are genuinely stuck.

Keyboard: D draw · H hint · Z undo · N new deal · E export · Esc close.

Sending a stack of four to a slot pays +10 a card, so gathering a group before its key turns up is worth more than banking one word at a time. The score never drops below zero.

Why two wins score differently. 720 of a win is fixed — four keys, 32 words, four completed categories and the +100 for finishing. The rest is how well you deduced:

  • Every wrong aim is −5, and one miss costs you the +150 perfect bonus outright.
  • Turning over face-down cards is worth up to +75.
  • Every extra trip through the stock costs −20.

In practice mode misses are free, but the perfect bonus is not awarded.
