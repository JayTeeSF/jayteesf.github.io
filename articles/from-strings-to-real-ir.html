Hey language · design note · 22 August 2026

From print-only strings to real IR

Hey steals Mojo's foundation, not its surface. Its three intermediate representations were print-only strings that later stages re-read character by character; the work is turning them into real, parseable, verifiable IRs that lower by structural rewriting — so an immutable, reference-counted language can update in place and reach C speed.
