Immediate critical path

Now: implement the non-returning scoped borrow consumer, migrate 213 local reader sites while preserving 257 owned escape sites, and produce candidate-shadow identity receipts in every execution lane. Only then can frontend authority switch.
Phase 1 — operator × position behavior 100/100 green

The last defect was LLVM dotted-field parsing: pair.left & pair.right was lowered as if .right applied to the whole binary expression. The shared receiver guard now recognizes top-level &, |, and ^. Interpreter, Stage-0, and LLVM-direct focused receipts pass.

Runway 0–7 — schema, generated construction, reads, and physical storage green
  • Stable node schema and generated projections.
  • Exact-sized generated construction.
  • Generated readers and child traversal.
  • Single physical n_ slot storage; no dual logical-key authority.
Runway 8A — indexed owned readers green

Stable numeric field IDs and indexed reads are active. Direct C borrowing has zero extra clones/string duplication, but active generated Hey readers still produce owned snapshots.

Runway 8B — scoped borrowed readers active

The C scope and generation-checked handle ABI are green. The missing piece is a compiler-recognized, non-returning consumer such as __compiler_node_with_borrow(node, field_id, consumer).

  • Begin immediately before synchronous consumer invocation.
  • End/invalidate on normal return, runtime error, early return, and every control-flow edge.
  • Allow only non-borrow results to leave; independent lifetime requires explicit promotion.
  • Migrate 213 local sites; keep 257 escape sites owned.

Exit receipt: zero local snapshot clones across interpreter, Stage-0, LLVM-direct, self-host, and REPL.

Runway 9 — candidate tokens and final syntax sidecar green

Candidate source management, tokenizer, generated final-syntax schema, parser, recovery, population, and names inventory exist. Ordinary emit-hir now executes tokens → syntax → names after authoritative HIR emission and can write a deterministic receipt. Candidate facts do not feed HIR.

Shadow identity gate — every lane and corpus red

The focused interpreter ordinary-build receipt is green and HIR bytes are unchanged. The complete compiled pipeline is large and did not finish within the sandbox command window, so no cross-lane identity claim is made.

Required: identical candidate facts and authoritative HIR hashes across interpreter, Stage-0, LLVM-direct, self-host, and REPL over the compiler corpus plus representative applications.

Runway 10 — names, scopes, modules, receiver/operator resolution partial

Name inventory is present; full authoritative resolution remains. It must preserve settled import/exposure behavior, receiver sugar, immutable bindings, and set only for privately owned state.

Runway 11 — structural HIR future

Replace raw source-fragment operands with structural syntax/name facts. The old frontend remains authoritative until shadow identity and borrowed-reader gates are green.

Runway 12 — mechanical MIR and LLVM future

Lower structural HIR into explicit machine/control-flow/ownership instructions so LLVM emission becomes mechanical rather than a second parser.

Runway 13 — shared interpreter and REPL future

Move interpreter and REPL onto the same HIR semantics used by compilation. This is required to prevent lane-specific language behavior.

Runway 14 / R8 — authority, deletion, and full self-host future

Switch authority, verify products, drive old counters to zero, and delete the legacy frontend/compatibility routes. Agent 30's zero-fallback LLVM-emitter build is already green, but that is not the same as a fully self-hosted authoritative frontend.
