The ownership and compiler-quadratics decision

The September 12 decision says that ordinary immutable Hey should not need a special “fast spelling.” The compiler should carry ownership facts through its intermediate representations, including when a function is passed to another function.

Reuse needs two facts: the storage has one owner, and nobody can observe the old logical value afterward. With those facts, MIR may authorize reusing the allocation. Without them, correct copying or copy-on-write preserves the program's meaning. Missing optimization proof is not itself a reason to reject a valid program.

A fold repeatedly passes its accumulator into a reducer and receives the next accumulator back. Passing that reducer as a callable must not accidentally lose a proven ownership contract. The proof must follow the resolved function identity, not its printed name. Code generation consumes the proof; it must not invent one.

Ownership can remove unnecessary copies. It cannot fix an algorithm that repeatedly scans everything it has already processed. Those repeated computations need indexes, summaries, or better traversals at the responsible compiler layer. Tests must distinguish the two costs, check aliases and unknown callables, and compare observable results with the safe path.

This is a public explanation of the decision, not the full internal document. See the ownership diagram and compiler-layer explanation. The complete archive, ordinary applications, and Linux acceptance remain separate requirements.

Scope of the September 15 status report

The three guides report focused compiler results, not product completion. Their original evidence snapshot runs through official commit 580a3ae2b3f138532f1c19c820bfcba1e4e94c3c. No complete ordinary-application or Linux performance certification is claimed. The private coordination brief is a record of that scope, not an additional independent experiment.

Runtime interpreter-service removal

Official commit 6bf118f1f63c93788299c7d3e6447aee62871761 removed the runtime standard-library interpreter service and its compiler route. Absence checks guard that removal. This does not prove every library provider compiles, nor that every other fallback has disappeared.

Floating-point self-tail calls

Commit e79b6e9f4cb72f9f25f7c83186c48cd7008f26a1 carries typed floating-point operations in the MIR tail-call plan. Commit f4d5356cab8abe367b666393477be0843b85c419 updates its generated compiler projection. Reported native Darwin arm64 checks reached 100,000 iterations with copy-on-write off and on; removing required plan, representation, or cleanup facts made the negative controls fail. This is a bounded correctness result, not a speed measurement.

Call-boundary checks

Commit 580a3ae2b3f138532f1c19c820bfcba1e4e94c3c records 29 passing focused assertions for composed calls and floating-point argument representation. An incompatible integer signature is rejected. These checks narrowed the Transformer investigation; they did not close its high-level parameter-inference defect.

Ownership through a known callable

Commit c482af47a593067779db6e99401e91cb4460d656 added a focused consume-and-return effect mechanism for known callable identities. Its specification checks MIR authorization and observable results with copy-on-write disabled, while ordinary indirect calls retain borrowed-input behavior. Full immutable-rebuild scaling and application performance are not established by that test.

The fold example

A reducer receives the current accumulator and the next item; its result becomes the next accumulator. The public runnable example adds four numbers and prints 10. This explains the call boundary without requiring access to the private library source.

Local actors

The public actor example receives two numbers, keeps their sum in private state, and prints 30. It checks local behavior, not multi-machine delivery.

Job supervision

The private supervision specification exercises a bounded retry flow and dead-letter observation. That is focused behavior, not a service-level or distributed-product guarantee. The full test source is not published here; the language guide explains the role of jobs.
