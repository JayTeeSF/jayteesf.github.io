Close the next boundary.

Hey has crossed several compiler and release thresholds, but a trustworthy product is measured by the first real consumer that still refuses. The current ladder separates complete work from evidence that is only a slice, then follows the shortest causal path to the next native program.

This is a dated status snapshot. It records the Cagents board and compiler evidence as of 5 September 2026. “Done” means the owning task's acceptance contract is fully closed. A candidate, focused gate, or green subcomponent stays open until its production consumer and negative controls are accepted.

The ladder

1 · Language surface

Hey has substantial functions, values, modules, actors, jobs, IO, networking, packages, and distributed transport. The language is useful enough that the remaining failures are mostly about carrying meaning through the compiler and runtime.

SUBSTANTIAL
2 · Semantic identity

Resolution now carries canonical call and module identities through generated projections and link rows. The distributed closure repair made the owning fact explicit: a Hey provider is compiled in because its own module declaration says so, not because a filename or spelling happens to match.

FOUNDATION CLOSED / CONSUMERS CONTINUE
3 · Self-hosted compiler trust

The release self-host build is strict and clean at the current frontier. The archive, stage-0 compiler, self-host compiler, and full runner all rebuild with fallback forbidden. That proves the build path; it does not make every application construct lowerable.

RELEASE FRONTIER GREEN
4 · Distributed native execution

The native actor fixture advanced past the former Node.start packaging failure after DistNode, DistRemote, and DistTransport became compiled-in. The next refusal is the child argument inside an imported boxed Io.read_exact call. Its focused candidate preserves child IR and uses the existing scalar boxing authority; native validation is still required.

NEXT CLOSURE
5 · Representation and ownership

The compiler still has open census and migration work where ownership or effects can be inferred from serialized MIR or printed spelling. The rule remains simple: resolution owns identity, HIR and MIR carry it, and LLVM consumes it without recovering semantics from text.

STRUCTURAL DEBT
6 · MAP_PUT and 1BRC

Several map and ownership slices are closed, but the production method-form update and real 1BRC closure remain open. The benchmark must prove that the language-level operation and the owned update share one semantic path with the claimed cost curve.

SPEED GATE OPEN
7 · FAST workloads

W12 has exact-result and native platform evidence: Darwin and Omarchy/Arch Linux both have strict release receipts. The broader FAST task still owns workload attribution, scan and allocation decomposition, ownership counters, and the remaining performance roadmap.

PARTIAL ACCEPTANCE
8 · Jobs, clustering, and durable state

Ordinary Jobs imports and several protocol/authority slices are integrated. hey_durable_log now proves local barriers plus opt-in real-peer quorum replication, healing, leader hand-off, fencing, and running-node partitions. The two-process native Jobs and actor acceptance gates still need to pass together, including placement, recovery, ownership, and failure semantics.

DURABILITY LANDed / PRODUCT OPEN
9 · Module authority and Sys cutover

The direction is established: ordinary modules own their operations, generated manifests project that ownership, and runtime facades remain explicit. The full cutover still requires application-corpus migration, legacy ambient-resolution hits at zero, and deletion of the old receiver tables.

CUTOVER OPEN
10 · Product closure

The final product is a friendly language with native costs close to the operation the programmer asked for, backed by reproducible release receipts. That requires the compiler, runtime, performance, release, and distributed gates to agree on the same source and identity—not a collection of isolated green dashboards.

NORTH STAR
The newest closure is specific. Commit 743be2c74 makes the Hey-owned distributed modules compiled-in through their own hey-module-abi declarations. The release propagation is fa7b1f2be. The focused module-closure gate proves canonical links, imported bodies, HIR ownership, and preserved Io/Result facade boundaries. The next native RED is now visible instead of being hidden behind a missing module body.

How the next work should run

The orchestrator should keep one heavyweight local transaction at a time, send static traces and focused fixtures to isolated agents, and integrate only candidates whose owning authority and negative controls are visible. Every compiler repair starts at the first zero-fallback failure, carries the resolved identity through the pipeline, preserves nested child IR, and reruns the real consumer before the task is called done.
